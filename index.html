Nightglide

A driving score that stays on your iPhone. Nightglide records your drives automatically, spots harsh braking, sharp acceleration, hard cornering, phone handling and speeding, and gives you a score out of 1000 with trends over time.

No account. No server. No analytics. Your drives are stored on your iPhone and nowhere else. Read the privacy policy for the detail.

Help

A drive wasn't recorded. Automatic recording needs location access set to Always: open the Settings app, then Privacy & Security, Location Services, Nightglide. Leave Nightglide in the app switcher rather than swiping it away, because iOS may stop waking apps that have been force-closed. In Nightglide, Trips > Missing a drive? shows a log of what the app saw.

The first part of a drive is missing. iOS takes a moment to wake the app after you set off, so very short trips can start a little late. You can also press Start drive before you leave.

A trip was recorded when I was a passenger. Open the trip and switch off "I was driving", or use the button on its card. It then stops counting towards your score.

How is the score worked out? Each category (braking, acceleration, cornering, phone handling, speed) starts at 1000 and loses points for events per mile driven over the period you choose. The overall score is the average of the five.

Moving to a new phone. Settings > Export all trips saves a file with every trip. Your trips are also part of your iPhone's own backup.

Safety

Don't use your phone while driving. Nightglide needs no attention on the road: look at your results once you've parked. Scores are estimates from your phone's sensors and are for your own interest only.
